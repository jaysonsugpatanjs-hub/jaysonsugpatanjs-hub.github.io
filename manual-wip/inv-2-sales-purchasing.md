## 0. Cross-cutting facts

### 0.1 Menu, routes, who sees what
Sidebar groups (`nav.side [data-nav]`, items `a.nav-item[href="#/..."]`):
- **Sales**: Customers `#/customers`, Quotes `#/quotes`, Invoices `#/invoices`, Payments received `#/receipts`. Visible to anyone holding any of `sales.manage`, `bank.manage`, `reports.view`.
- **Purchases**: Suppliers `#/suppliers`, Purchase orders `#/purchase-orders` (any of `purchases.manage`, `purchases.raise`, `bank.manage`, `reports.view`); Bills `#/bills`, Supplier payments `#/supplier-payments` (any of `purchases.manage`, `bank.manage`, `reports.view`; NOT `purchases.raise`).
- Credit notes (sales) have no menu item: `#/invoices?kind=credit_note` (button **Credit notes** on the Invoices page). Supplier credits: `#/bills?kind=credit_note` (button **Supplier credits** on the Bills page).
- Payment batches (`#/payment-batches`, group Banking, needs `bank.manage`) is the other way to pay bills.
- Sub-routes: `#/customers/new`, `/<id>`, `/<id>/edit`; same pattern for quotes, invoices, suppliers, purchase-orders, bills. Receipts and supplier payments: `/new`, `/<id>` only (no edit). Query params: `#/invoices/new?customer=<id>`, `?kind=credit_note&from=<invoice id>`, `?project=<id>`; `#/quotes/new?customer=`, `?project=`; `#/bills/new?supplier=`, `?po=<po id>`, `?kind=credit_note`, `?project=`; `#/purchase-orders/new?supplier=`, `?project=`; `#/receipts/new?customer=&invoice=`; `#/supplier-payments/new?supplier=&bill=`. List filters via hash: `#/quotes?status=accepted`, `#/invoices?view=overdue`, `#/bills?view=unpaid`.
- Direct URL without the right permission silently redirects to `#/dashboard`.

### 0.2 Permissions (keys, display names, who holds them)
Display names (appear in "You need the "X" permission for this."): `sales.manage` = "Sales"; `purchases.manage` = "Purchases"; `purchases.raise` = "Raise purchase orders"; `purchases.bank` = "Approve supplier bank changes"; `bank.manage` = "Banking"; `reports.view` = "Financial reports".
MFA is required at sign-in (authenticator gate "Set up two-step sign-in" / "Confirm it's you") for anyone holding sales.manage, purchases.manage, purchases.bank, bank.manage (and other privileged keys). `purchases.raise`, `projects.manage`, `reports.view`, `time.approve` do not need MFA, so a project manager signs in without it.

### 0.4 No separation of duties inside a document
The same person (any holder of purchases.manage) can enter, approve and pay (with bank.manage) a bill; sales.manage creates and approves invoices. Only these things need a second person in this area: supplier bank-detail changes (purchases.bank holder, not the requester), payment batches (banking module). There is no "approval request" for invoices, bills or POs: they are status buttons on the document. The Approvals inbox (`#/approvals`) only holds supplier_bank requests from this area. Dashboard shows counts of bills to review and POs to approve.

### 0.5 Numbering (Company settings > numbering; defaults prefix/start/pad in seed: INV- 1001/4, QU- 1001/4, PO- 1001/4, CN- 1001/4, BILL- 1001/4, journals JE- 6 digits)
- Quote number QU-nnnn: given when the draft is first saved. PO number PO-nnnn: on first save. Bill number BILL-nnnn: on first save; supplier credits share the BILL- sequence. Deleting a draft bill/PO leaves a gap.
- Invoice INV-nnnn and credit note CN-nnnn: given on approval only (drafts show "Draft"); a voided number stays used.
- Journal numbers JE-nnnnnn link from the document ("Ledger" row).

### 0.6 Line item editor (shared by quote, invoice, credit note, PO, bill, supplier credit)
`form[data-doc]`. Header grid: party select `#d-party` (label "Customer *" or "Supplier *", placeholder "Choose a customer…" / "Choose a supplier…"; only ACTIVE parties listed), document-specific fields, then **Amounts are** `#d-amounts` with three options:
- "Tax exclusive (GST added)" (default), "Tax inclusive (GST included)", "No GST" (disables every line's tax select, tax codes dropped).
Line table `table.lines.doc-lines`, columns: Description | Account | Qty | Unit price | Disc % | Tax | GST | Amount | (remove). Per line also three small selects under the description: Type (Labour, Materials, Equipment, Subcontract, Travel, Consumables, Freight, Other), **Project** ("No project" + `JOB-number name`; cancelled/closed projects not offered unless already on the line), and on PO/bill/supplier-credit only a **Cost code** select ("Cost code…", disabled until a project is chosen). Sales documents have project tag but no cost code. Selectors: `tr[data-line="0"]`, inputs by `[data-f="description|kind|projectId|costCodeId|accountId|quantity|unitPrice|discountPercent|taxCodeId"]`, aria-labels "Description, line 1", "Account, line 1", "Quantity, line 1", "Unit price, line 1", "Discount percent, line 1", "Tax code, line 1", "Project, line 1", "Cost code, line 1". Remove = `button[data-remove]` (✕, disabled when only one line). Add = `button[data-add-line]` "+ Add a line" (copies previous line's account, tax, project, cost code). Live totals `dl.totals[data-totals]`: "Subtotal (ex GST)" (or "Subtotal" when No GST), "GST", "Total". Invalid qty/price/discount get `.invalid` and totals say "Check the highlighted amounts".
- Account list: sales documents offer active revenue and other-income accounts (allow-manual) grouped by type headings (e.g. "Revenue": 4000 Fabrication Revenue, 4100 Welding Revenue, 4200 Shutdown/Maintenance Revenue, 4300 Pipework Revenue, 4400 Site Labour Revenue, 4500 Labour Hire Revenue, 4600 Equipment Hire Revenue, 4700 Materials Recharge, 4800 Other Operating Revenue). Purchase documents offer everything active and manual except revenue, other income and equity (cost of sales 5000-5700, expenses, assets such as 1200 Inventory, 1500 Tools and Equipment, liability accounts that are manual). Control accounts (1100 Accounts Receivable, 2000 Accounts Payable) are never offered.
- Default account: customer's default income account, else 4000 Fabrication Revenue; supplier's default expense account, else 5100 Direct Materials. Default tax: party default tax code, else the chosen account's default (4xxx = GST; 5100-5700 = GSTE; 1500/1600/1700/1800 = CAP). Picking an account fills a blank tax code from the account.
- Tax codes offered: sales side (applies_to sales or both): GST (10%), FRE (GST-free income), EXP (GST-free exports), ITS (input taxed sales), NG (no GST), OOS (out of scope). Purchase side (purchases or both): GSTE (10%), CAP (10%, capital), FREE (GST-free expenses), ITP (input taxed purchases), NG, OOS. Only the code appears in the line select; the customer/supplier default-tax selects show "CODE · name".
- Maths (browser mirrors SQL): amount = round(qty x unit price x (1 - disc%/100), 2) half-up; GST exclusive = round(amount x rate, 2); inclusive = round(amount x rate / (1+rate), 2) and subtotal = amount - GST. Per-line rounding. Qty up to 3 decimals, price up to 4, discount 0-100. Up to 150 lines.
- Submit-time checks in the browser (shown in the red status line `p.msg[data-msg]`): "Choose a customer." / "Choose a supplier."; "Fix the highlighted amounts first."; "Add at least one line."; "Line N: add a description and choose an account." (A brand-new form already has the default account preselected, so saving it untouched gives "Line 1: add a description and choose an account." because the description is blank.) Buttons are disabled while saving and re-enabled on error.
- SQL line errors (all prefixed "Line N: " — N counts the lines as sent): "quantity, price and discount must be numbers."; "quantity must be more than 0, with up to 3 decimals."; "unit price can't be negative and has up to 4 decimals."; "discount is between 0 and 100%."; "add a description."; "choose an account."; "account 4xxx can't be used here." (archived or control); "use an income account for sales (CODE is type)."; "use an expense, cost or asset account for purchases."; "unknown or inactive tax code."; "that tax code is for purchases, not sales." (and the reverse: "for sales, not purchases."); "that project was cancelled or doesn't exist."; "unknown or inactive cost code."; "choose the project the cost code belongs to." Document level: "Choose how amounts are entered."; "Add at least one line."; "A document can have at most 150 lines."
- Zero-value lines (e.g. 100% discount "no charge") stay on the document but are left out of the journal.

### 0.7 Statuses (display label, chip colour)
Invoice/credit note `INVOICE_STATUS`: draft "Draft" (pending/amber), approved "Approved" (good/green), void "Void" (grey).
Quote `QUOTE_STATUS`: draft "Draft", approved "Approved" (info/blue), sent "Sent" (blue), accepted "Accepted" (green), declined "Declined" (red), converted "Invoiced" (green), cancelled "Cancelled" (grey). Computed chip "Expired" (red) when valid-until date < today and status is draft/approved/sent.
PO `PO_STATUS`: draft "Draft", submitted "Awaiting approval" (amber), approved "Approved" (blue), issued "Issued" (blue), partially_received "Part received" (blue), completed "Completed" (green), cancelled "Cancelled".
Bill/supplier credit `BILL_STATUS`: draft "Draft", submitted "For review", approved "Approved", void "Void".
Payments: customer receipts show "Banked" (green) or "Void"; supplier payments "Paid" (green) or "Void" (DB value `posted`).
Computed (not stored) chips: "Paid" (approved, owing 0; credit notes/supplier credits say "Applied" in lists and "Fully applied" in detail), "Overdue" (approved invoice/bill with owing > 0 and due date before today; list shows "N days overdue" under the due date for invoices), "Sent" (invoice has sent_at), "Over limit" (customer balance > credit limit; warning only, invoicing is NOT blocked), "No ABN", "Subcontractor", "TPAR", "Expired", "N days" (supplier insurance/licence), "Change waiting for approval", "Archived".

### 0.8 Ledger posting summary (engine `ledger_post_entry`; GST all goes to one account 2300 "GST Payable/Receivable"; AR 1100; AP 2000; withholding 2100 "PAYG Withholding Payable")
| Event | Debit | Credit | Journal date / memo / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice approved | 1100 AR (total) | each income line (ex GST) and 2300 GST | invoice date; "Invoice INV-nnnn"; source invoice |
| Credit note approved | income lines and 2300 GST | 1100 AR | credit note date; "Credit note CN-nnnn" |
| Receipt recorded | chosen bank account (Paid into) | 1100 AR | date received; "Receipt from <customer> · <ref>"; type customer_payment |
| Bill approved | each expense/cost/asset line (ex GST) and 2300 GST | 2000 AP (total minus withholding), 2100 PAYG (withholding, if any) | bill date; "Bill BILL-nnnn · <their ref> · <supplier>" |
| Supplier credit approved | 2000 AP (total) | expense lines and 2300 GST | "Supplier credit BILL-nnnn ..." |
| Supplier payment | 2000 AP | chosen bank account (Paid from) | payment date; "Payment to <supplier> · <ref>" |
| Void (invoice, bill, receipt, payment) | exact mirror of the original journal, dated the original date | | original status becomes "reversed", linked "reversed by JE-..." |
Quotes, POs, drafts, "sent" flags and credit/payment applications post nothing. Allocations (apply credit, apply payment) move no ledger money; they only change "owing".
Posting errors that can appear on any approve/record/void: "No accounting period covers 08 Oct 2026. Add that financial year first." / "The period 01 Jul 2026 to 31 Aug 2026 is closed." / "The period ... is locked. Only someone who can reopen periods can post into it." (soft-locked periods accept only holders of ledger.reopen, i.e. director, super admin) / "There is no active GST account." Voiding uses the ORIGINAL document date, so a void of an item dated in a locked period fails the same way. Bank-matched cash entries: "This entry is matched to a bank statement line. Unmatch it in Reconciliation first, or record a refund instead." (blocks receipt/payment voids after the bank line is matched/reconciled).
Manual journal reversal of a document's journal is refused: "This journal was posted by a bill. Void the bill itself instead, so the two stay in step." (type name inserted, e.g. "invoice", "customer payment").
GST basis: company is set to cash or accrual in Company settings; BAS reads payment allocations on cash basis (not changed here).

Please try again.", "The upload didn't arrive. Please try again.", "A secure upload link could not be created.", "The file could not be opened.", "Attachment not found.", 403 text above. Stored in private bucket finance-documents under org/<org>/<entity>/<id>/. Typical use: supplier's invoice PDF on a bill, signed PO, remittance. Nothing requires an attachment before approval.

### 0.10 PDFs and email
- Buttons **Download PDF** (`button[data-pdf]`): quote -> `Quote-QU-1001.pdf`; invoice -> `Tax-invoice-INV-nnnn.pdf` (title "Tax invoice"; becomes "Invoice" if Company settings say not registered for GST); credit note -> `Adjustment-note-CN-nnnn.pdf` (title "Adjustment note"; shows the invoice it adjusts); PO -> `Purchase-order-PO-nnnn.pdf`; customer statement -> `Statement-<Customer>-<to date>.pdf`. Drafts print "DRAFT - NOT YET ISSUED"; void prints "VOID". Bills, receipts, payments have no PDF. Each generation is audit-logged (`document_pdf_generated`). Invoice PDF shows seller name/ABN/ACN/address/logo, customer name and ABN, bank account (the company bank account approved with "show on invoices"), notes, terms, GST, `*` marks lines with no GST, paid and balance.
- NOTHING IS EMAILED. "Mark as sent" only records the date/time and shows a "Sent" chip; the "Accounts email" fields are stored for later. The staff member downloads the PDF and emails it from their own mail.

### 0.11 Duplicate detection (searched deliberately)
- Customers: NO duplicate check on name or ABN (only ABN check-digit validity). Two customers with the same name/ABN can be saved; the committed test data even reuses ABN 51 824 753 556 for several customers and suppliers.
- Suppliers: NO duplicate check on name or ABN.
- Supplier invoice number: YES, enforced on save of a bill (not a soft warning; it blocks the save). SQL: `A bill with supplier reference <ref> already exists for this supplier.` Case-insensitive, trimmed, same supplier only, ignores void bills, includes drafts, for-review, approved and supplier credits (kind not considered, so a supplier credit note cannot reuse an invoice number). Blank reference is never a duplicate. Field label: "Supplier's invoice number". Not checked across suppliers.
- Invoice customer PO number: no uniqueness check.
- A quote can only be turned into one live invoice (see quotes).

### 8.4 Payment batches vs supplier payments (how bills get paid)
Two routes, both end as the same "Supplier payment" records and journals:
1. Manual: **Pay bills** (this page), or the **Pay** button on a bill; record payments already made (online banking, cheque, card). Immediate, single user (bank.manage).
2. Payment batch (`#/payment-batches`, bank.manage): **New batch** picks approved bills (supplier must have an approved bank account, 9-digit max account number); the batch (PB-nnnn, status draft) must be **Approve**d by a DIFFERENT person ("Someone other than the person who made the batch must approve it."); then **Download bank file (ABA)**; after the bank processes it, **Mark as paid** (only after the file was downloaded: "Download the bank file and send it to the bank first.") creates one supplier payment per supplier, reference = batch number, paid from the batch's linked ledger account. While a bill is in an open batch it cannot be paid by hand or voided (guard messages above). A batch can be **Cancel batch**ed with a reason; once a file is downloaded only someone other than the downloader may cancel.
3. Bank reconciliation: a bank statement line can create the supplier payment ("pay bills") and match it in one step (banking module).

## 9. Credit notes, overpayments and allocation: quick reference
- Sales credit note: new via invoice's **Credit note** button or Invoices > **Credit notes** > **New credit note**; approve (CN number, Dr income/GST, Cr AR). It shows as owing "Left to apply". Apply to an invoice from the credit note page (**Apply to an invoice**) or from the invoice page (**Apply existing credit**). Unapplied credit reduces the customer's balance and shows under ageing "Credits". There is no refund function in the UI.
- Customer overpayment: remainder after allocation stays on the receipt ("Not applied"); apply later with **Apply the credit** on the receipt or **Apply existing credit** on the invoice. Voiding the receipt removes its allocations and reopens the invoices.
- Supplier credit: **Supplier credits** > **New supplier credit** > approve; apply with the bill's **Supplier credit** form.
- All allocations: cannot exceed what is left on either side; dated no earlier than either document; ageing reports honour the allocation dates. "Paid"/"Overdue" are computed from outstanding amounts.

## 12. Surprises and behaviours worth a verification note
1. No duplicate detection for customer/supplier names or ABNs; only duplicate supplier invoice number is blocked (hard error on save, not a warning).
2. Invoice "Apply existing credit" shows to sales-only users, but applying an unallocated RECEIPT needs bank.manage.
3. No cap on credit notes vs the original invoice, and no UI to un-apply a credit note. An invoice with an applied credit note can't be voided (and neither can the credit note).
4. Voiding any document or receipt/payment reverses on the original date, so a locked/closed period blocks the void; and bank-matched receipts/payments cannot be voided until unmatched.
5. Quote -> invoice takes all lines; no partial quoting; quote stays "Invoiced" even if that invoice is later voided; no quote -> project conversion in the UI.
6. Invoice drafts and unapproved bills are physically deleted (Delete draft / Delete), not marked void; bill numbers are used at first save, so gaps appear.
7. Same person can create and approve invoices, bills, and POs (if they hold purchases.manage). Only supplier bank changes and payment batches need a second person.
8. No-ABN withholding is 47% of the GST-inclusive TOTAL (not ex-GST), applies to bills over $75 ex GST, nothing for supplier credits; supplier payments pay only the net.
9. Supplier payments cannot be over-allocated or left unallocated (no prepayment); customer receipts can.
10. Nothing is emailed anywhere ("Mark as sent" is just a flag).
11. PO: goods can only be receipted when status is Issued or Part received; "Approved" must be followed by "Mark as issued to supplier" before receiving. A bill can be raised from an Approved PO even if nothing has been received (then bills the ordered quantity and the match panel flags "Check").
12. Purchase tax-code select on a sale (or sales code on a purchase) is blocked by SQL even if forced; sales lines also accept liability accounts server-side, but the UI never offers them.
13. Sales pages are visible read-only to project managers, payroll admins and accountants via reports.view; purchasing "Bills" and "Supplier payments" menu entries are hidden for purchases.raise-only users but visible to reports.view holders.
14. Bank account numbers on the supplier detail and supplier payment pages are masked (•••678) only for users without bank.manage and purchases.bank; finance, director, sysadmin see them in full, so screenshots need masking.
15. The bill editor label for a supplier credit is still "Supplier's invoice number" and credit notes share the BILL- number sequence.
