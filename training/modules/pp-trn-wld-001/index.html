LEARNING OBJECTIVES

At the end of this training, the employee should be able to…

  • Identify MIG welding and grinding hazards.
  • Select and inspect PPE and equipment.
  • Confirm the applicable permit, SWMS/JSA and fire-watch arrangements.
  • Apply ventilation, exclusion, stop-work and emergency controls.
  • Recognise quality and material traceability controls.
