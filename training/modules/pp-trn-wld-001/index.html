PP-TRN-WLD-001

Hot Work – MIG Welding and Grinding Safety

Interactive online learning for MIG welding, tack welding, grinding and hot-metal handling. The lesson sequence follows the approved training PowerPoint structure used as the reference for this pilot.

Target rolesBoilermakers / Welders / Trade Assistants as applicable
DeliveryOnline learning + online theory check + onsite verification
Duration30–45 min online + onsite demonstration
PracticalRequired
Theory pass80% overall + 100% critical questions
Final statusTheory pass does not equal practical authorisation

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

At the end of this training, the employee should be able to...

  • Identify MIG welding and grinding hazards.
  • Select and inspect PPE and equipment.
  • Apply hierarchy-of-control principles.
  • Follow SWMS/JSA, stop-work and emergency steps.
  • Recognise quality and traceability controls.
