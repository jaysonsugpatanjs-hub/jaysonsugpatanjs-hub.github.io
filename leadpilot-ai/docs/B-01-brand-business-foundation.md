# B-01 — LeadPilot AI Brand & Business Foundation

**Project:** LeadPilot AI — AI Lead Generation & CRM Automation System  
**Milestone:** B — Brand & Business Foundation  
**Status:** Complete  
**Basis:** LeadPilot AI A–Z Implementation Guide 2026

## 1. Brand position

**LeadPilot AI helps B2B SMEs create a measurable sales pipeline by connecting website lead capture, CRM, AI qualification, workflow automation and structured follow-up.**

The brand is positioned as a practical B2B growth and automation service, not as a generic AI consultancy. The promise is operational: improve how leads are captured, organized, qualified, followed up and measured.

## 2. Target market

LeadPilot AI is designed initially for B2B small and medium-sized businesses, with the demonstration workflow focused on industrial services.

Starter verticals suitable for the portfolio build include:

- Industrial services
- Fabrication and welding
- Maintenance and shutdown contractors
- Engineering subcontractors
- Construction subcontractors
- Labour hire / specialist trades

The CRM architecture should remain generic enough to support other B2B SMEs later.

## 3. Core customer problem

Target customers often have some combination of these problems:

- Website enquiries arrive but are not consistently followed up.
- Leads are stored across inboxes, spreadsheets and individual notes.
- Sales teams cannot quickly identify which leads deserve priority.
- CRM usage is inconsistent or incomplete.
- Follow-up tasks depend on memory.
- Management has poor visibility over pipeline quality and progress.

## 4. Starter offer — Version 1

The MVP offer intentionally includes only the components needed to prove the system works.

| Component | MVP | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot CRM setup | Yes | Creates the core system of record. |
| Website inquiry capture | Yes | Provides real inbound lead-capture proof. |
| AI lead qualification | Yes | Demonstrates the AI differentiator. |
| n8n follow-up/task automation | Yes | Demonstrates practical workflow automation. |
| Basic reporting | Yes | Connects the technical build to business value. |

## 5. Deferred services

These are deliberately excluded from the beginner MVP and may be added later:

- Paid ads management
- AI voice calling
- Mass outbound automation

They are deferred because they add advertising spend, creative testing, compliance, quality-control, reputation and operational complexity that is unnecessary for the first portfolio version.

## 7. Value proposition

LeadPilot AI is not selling “AI” by itself. It is selling a better sales operating process.

**Before:**

`Enquiry → Inbox → Spreadsheet / Notes → Manual Review → Inconsistent Follow-up → Limited Visibility`

**After:**

`Enquiry → HubSpot CRM → n8n Validation → AI Qualification → Priority → Follow-up Task → Pipeline Reporting`

## 8. Customer outcomes

The starter offer is intended to help a client achieve:

- Faster lead response
- More consistent lead follow-up
- Centralized contact and company information
- Clear lead prioritization
- Reduced manual administration
- Better visibility of opportunities
- Traceable sales activity
- A foundation for future automation

## 11. Before vs after customer journey

### Before LeadPilot AI

1. Prospect submits an enquiry or contacts the business.
2. Information lands in an inbox or spreadsheet.
3. Someone manually checks the enquiry.
4. Priority is based on personal judgment or memory.
5. Follow-up may be delayed or forgotten.
6. Management has limited pipeline visibility.

### After LeadPilot AI

1. Prospect submits an enquiry.
2. Lead data enters HubSpot CRM.
3. n8n validates and routes the data.
4. OpenAI assesses fit, urgency and available evidence.
5. Lead receives an explainable score and priority.
6. A recommended next action or follow-up task is created.
7. Sales activity and opportunity progress can be reported.
