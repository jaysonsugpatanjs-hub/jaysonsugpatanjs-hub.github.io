Project objective

Build a real, testable AI-enabled lead-generation operating system — not only a landing page, spreadsheet or collection of prompts.

The business problem

Small B2B service companies can lose opportunities when enquiries are scattered across website forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up.

The proposed solution

LeadPilot AI captures leads, stores them in a structured CRM, uses AI to assess fit and urgency, recommends the next action, and supports traceable pipeline reporting.

B — Brand & Business Foundation · Complete

What LeadPilot AI sells

LeadPilot AI is positioned as a practical B2B sales-process and automation service, not a generic AI consultancy.

Starter positioning

LeadPilot AI helps B2B SMEs create a measurable sales pipeline by connecting website lead capture, CRM, AI qualification, workflow automation and structured follow-up.

Target market

B2B SMEs, initially demonstrated through industrial services, fabrication, maintenance, engineering subcontractors and related service businesses.

Customer outcome

Turn scattered enquiries into a structured, prioritized and measurable sales workflow with faster and more consistent follow-up.

✓ Starter offer — MVP

  • HubSpot CRM setup
  • Website inquiry capture
  • AI lead qualification
  • n8n follow-up/task automation
  • Basic reporting

Deferred until later

  • Paid ads management
  • AI voice calling
  • Mass outbound automation

These add spend, compliance, reputation and operational complexity that the beginner MVP does not need.

Simple elevator pitch

LeadPilot AI helps B2B SMEs turn scattered enquiries into a structured sales pipeline by connecting lead capture, HubSpot CRM, AI qualification and automated follow-up.

Before vs after customer journey

The business value becomes clearer when the operating process is compared side by side.

Before LeadPilot AI

1. Enquiry arrives
2. Inbox / spreadsheet
3. Manual review
4. Priority based on memory
5. Follow-up may be delayed
6. Limited pipeline visibility

After LeadPilot AI

1. Enquiry captured
2. HubSpot CRM
3. n8n validates/routes
4. AI qualification
5. Score + priority + next action
6. Follow-up + pipeline reporting
