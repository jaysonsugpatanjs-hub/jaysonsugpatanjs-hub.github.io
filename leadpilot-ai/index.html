Project objective

Build a real, testable AI-enabled lead-generation operating system — not only a landing page, spreadsheet or collection of prompts.

The business problem

Small B2B service companies can lose opportunities when enquiries are scattered across website forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up.

The proposed solution

LeadPilot AI captures leads, stores them in a structured CRM, uses AI to assess fit and urgency, recommends the next action, and supports traceable pipeline reporting.

A — Agency Objective

Foundation implemented

Milestone A defines the project before automation begins: who the system is for, what problem it solves, what Version 1 includes, and how MVP success will be measured.

Official objective

LeadPilot AI is a portfolio project designed to demonstrate how a B2B service company can combine website lead capture, HubSpot CRM, n8n workflow automation and OpenAI-powered lead qualification to create a structured and measurable sales pipeline.

Demo client

Northstar Industrial Services
Fictional industrial-services company in Victoria, Australia.

Primary users

Business owner, business development, sales and administration staff.

MVP success

One demo inquiry completes the full capture → CRM → AI qualification → priority → follow-up flow reliably.

✓ Version 1 includes

  • Website inquiry capture
  • HubSpot contact and company records
  • Lead source and basic opportunity tracking
  • n8n workflow automation
  • OpenAI lead qualification
  • Explainable AI score and priority
  • Recommended next action
  • Follow-up task or equivalent action
  • Basic reporting, QA and portfolio evidence

Version 1 excludes

  • Mass cold-email automation
  • AI voice calling
  • Paid advertising management
  • Large-scale web scraping
  • Fully autonomous sales decisions
  • Complex multi-client SaaS architecture
  • Advanced billing/subscriptions
  • Confidential production client data
