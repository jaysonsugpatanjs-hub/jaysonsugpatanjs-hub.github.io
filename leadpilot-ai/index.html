Project objective

Build a real, testable AI-enabled lead-generation operating system — not just a landing page or a collection of prompts.

The business problem

Small B2B service companies can lose opportunities when enquiries are scattered across forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up.

The portfolio solution

LeadPilot AI captures leads, stores them in a structured CRM, uses AI to assess fit and urgency, recommends the next action, and supports traceable pipeline reporting.

MVP workflow

The first working version stays intentionally small: one new inquiry should move cleanly through capture, CRM, AI qualification and follow-up.
