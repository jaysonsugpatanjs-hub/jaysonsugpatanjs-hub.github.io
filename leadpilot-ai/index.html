Project objective

Build a real, testable AI-enabled lead-generation operating system — not only a landing page, spreadsheet or collection of prompts.

The business problem

Small B2B service companies can lose opportunities when enquiries are scattered across website forms, inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up.

The proposed solution

LeadPilot AI captures leads, stores them in a structured CRM, uses AI to assess fit and urgency, recommends the next action, and supports traceable pipeline reporting.

What LeadPilot AI sells

LeadPilot AI is positioned as a practical B2B sales-process and automation service, not a generic AI consultancy.

Starter positioning

LeadPilot AI helps B2B SMEs create a measurable sales pipeline by connecting website lead capture, CRM, AI qualification, workflow automation and structured follow-up.

Starter offer

  • HubSpot CRM setup
  • Website inquiry capture
  • AI lead qualification
  • n8n follow-up/task automation
  • Basic reporting

Who LeadPilot AI is designed to prioritize

The CRM stays generic, but the demonstration ICP is specific enough that later data fields, AI scoring and qualification rules can be tested against real criteria.

Starter demonstration ICP

AttributeStarter rule
RegionVictoria, Australia
Business typeIndustrial service, fabrication, maintenance, engineering subcontractor
Company size5–100 employees
Buyer rolesOwner, General Manager, Operations Manager, Maintenance Manager, Procurement / Projects
Typical painInconsistent enquiries, slow follow-up, weak CRM discipline, tender opportunities not tracked
High-value signalsActive projects, shutdown capability, recurring maintenance demand, multiple service lines
